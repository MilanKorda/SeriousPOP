SeriousPOP – Module 1: “Integers as sums of squares”

Drag square tiles onto the board. Your goal: match the target integer as a sum of square areas.

  • Drag tiles from the palette (right side) into the board (left).
  • Tiles snap to the grid. They must fit and not overlap.
  • Press R to reset board. Press N for a new target.
  • Double-click a placed tile to remove it.
